## 🛸 Who We Are

We're a student-led community at **Gokaraju Rangaraju Institute of Engineering and Technology (GRIET)** exploring everything that flies.

We build, fly, experiment, compete, and learn together.

From electronics and embedded systems to UAV design, communication, control systems, CAD, and autonomous flight — our goal is simple:

> **Learn by building.**

Whether you're already building drones or just curious about how they work, there's a place for you here.

---

## 🔧 What We Do

### 🛠️ Build

We design and build our own drones, UAV systems, electronics and experimental platforms.

### 🚁 Fly

We test, tune and fly our builds — because nothing beats seeing something you've built take off.

### 🏆 Compete

We participate in drone, robotics, engineering competitions and ideathons.

### 💡 Innovate

We turn ideas into prototypes and prototypes into working systems.

### 🧑‍🏫 Conduct Workshops

We share what we learn through hands-on workshops, technical sessions and ideathons.

### 🤝 Collaborate

We work across disciplines and encourage members to build together.
